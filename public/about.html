First step for every player
Before you press play

Enable audio first

Browsers wait for a real user click before allowing sound. Every person in the room should press Enable audio once after joining.

  1. Open Ambient ForgeSelect the extension from the Owlbear Rodeo toolbar.
  2. Press Enable audioThe button turns green when that browser is ready to play sound.
  3. Ask every listener to do itEach player enables audio on their own device. After that, synchronized scenes can be heard normally.

Create your first scene

Give every moment its own look

Create a scene, name it, choose an emoji, and pick a color that matches the mood. The same visual system works across folders and scenes, so a large library stays easy to scan.

  • Create scenes directly inside the selected folder.
  • Choose from a full, categorized emoji picker.
  • Set a custom accent color for quick recognition.
  • Adjust the scene volume and fade behavior before saving.
Layered audio

Two tracks—or as many as the scene needs

Combine music with ambience, voices, or effects in the same scene. Every track keeps its own source, name, volume, mute state, loop, and delay settings.

  • Add two or more independent tracks to one scene.
  • Loop music and ambience continuously.
  • Use randomized delay for occasional effects.
  • Set scene-wide fade in and fade out.

Good to know

Quick answers

Does audio continue when I close the extension popover?

Yes. Ambient Forge keeps playback running while the popover is closed, so it does not need to occupy your screen during play.

Can players set their own listening volume?

Yes. The game master controls the mix, while each player can adjust the master listening level on their own device.

Where are my folders and scenes saved?

They are saved automatically in that browser. Export an .aforge backup if you plan to clear browser data, switch browsers, or move to another device.

Can I prepare sounds without broadcasting them?

Yes. Select Only me to test privately, then switch to Everyone when you are ready for the table.
